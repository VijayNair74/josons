Our story

Generations on the spice route.

Handed down from father to son, the family's spice trade in Aluva has carried the Josons name since 1995. We buy from growers across Kerala, sort every lot by hand, and supply buyers who need consistent quality at a fair price.

"Every lot that leaves Aluva carries the Josons name. We would not send you anything we would not buy ourselves." — Manu Dominic Parakkal, Proprietor
